# Design Bible

## World Rules
- All combat interactions are framed actions — no analog damage drift
- Physics is deterministic integer/fixed-point where floating point would cause drift
- The world has no random events without seeded entropy

## Combat Philosophy
- Offense and defense are symmetric in execution cost
- Cancel windows are earned through frame discipline
- Powder Focus is a transformative state, not a passive buff

## Player Experience Model
- Skill expression lives in movement and cancel routing
- Dojo rules are the social contract of each match context
- Spectating is a first-class feature, not an afterthought

## Narrative Constraints
- World is stylized, not hyper-real
- Aesthetic references: ink, cloth, powder, structured motion
- No gore — combat resolution is state-based, not visceral
